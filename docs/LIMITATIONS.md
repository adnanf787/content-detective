# Interpretation, ethics, and limitations

- **Data status:** the official API pilot contains 30 real YouTube videos and 279 sampled top-level public comments. The separate demo is synthetic. Only brand-account affiliation is verified through corporate links; viewer geography is unknown. Instagram real-data acquisition is deferred.
- **Pakistan relevance:** account location is a Pakistan-market proxy. Audience location must come from independent evidence; language and brand category are not demographics.
- **Grain:** the post uploader expects one row per post. The YouTube pilot has a separate linked comment table; total public comment counts are not the downloaded sample size. Per-post multiple audience segments and segment-specific exposure tables are not implemented.
- **Comparability:** raw likes/comments/views differ across platforms. Rate denominators have different definitions. Available interactions may contain different components. Inspect exact definitions, coverage, dates, and observation age.
- **Missingness:** unknown counts remain missing. Complete-interaction rates may exclude many rows. Recommendations require ≥80% group coverage, but even that does not guarantee a representative sample.
- **Confounding:** account size, campaign spend, paid boosts, post age, account identity, and collection selection can explain associations. No causal effect or predicted lift is claimed.
- **Timing:** source calendar-date filters differ from Karachi-local dates. Naive unknown-zone timestamps cannot support local scheduling; date-only observations cannot support hours.
- **Sentiment:** caption sentiment and public-comment cue rules are separate and unevaluated. Only 23/279 sampled comments received sentiment cues; 256 are unassessed. Sarcasm, mixed language, Roman Urdu, Urdu, context, and negation remain limitations. No accuracy, macro-F1, calibrated confidence, or representative audience sentiment is claimed without supplied human labels.
- **Popularity:** hashtag usage frequency describes the uploaded account posts, not nationwide trends or audience demand.
- **Duplicates:** identical original rows are removed with counts. Conflicting source/account/post records are retained but excluded. Local IDs are references only. If IDs/namespace fields are missing, review quality manually.
- **Statistics:** minimum n=5 is configurable and exploratory; no power calculation, p-values, confidence percentages, or hypothesis testing are provided. A top median may be tied or confounded.
- **Privacy and collection:** official YouTube collection persists local raw JSON/CSV/SQLite and does not request author-profile fields or replies. Comment text can still contain self-disclosed details. Raw CSV uploads/manual staging stay in session memory. No external AI service is called. Review text and source terms before sharing exports.
- **Pilot selection:** the common window was extended to October 2025–September 2026 with user approval because Zong had only seven videos in the original interval. Newest 10 per brand is a convenience sample with different post ages, not a representative census. Comment sampling selects up to 30 newest visible top-level comments per video and is affected by moderation and deletion. A cached resume does not refresh measurements.
- **Scale:** CSV imports are limited to 25 MB and 200 columns. This is a student laptop MVP, not a distributed scraping or analytics service.
