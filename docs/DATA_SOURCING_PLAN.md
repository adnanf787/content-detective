## Feasible collection workflow

1. Choose one niche (fashion/retail, food, or e-commerce) and 2–3 actual Pakistani brands. Prefer authorized official post-level exports. Ask the course instructor whether a small well-documented pilot is acceptable for the available deadline.
2. Aim for 3 brands × 2 platforms × 25–40 posts (150–240) only if reliable collection is practical. A smaller, transparent sample is preferable to unsupported data. A single platform may improve comparability and reduce collection effort.
3. Use the downloadable collection template. Record source URL/account, source type, posting timestamp and timezone evidence, observed counts, collection date, and content type. Optional topics should use a consistent human annotation guide. Unknown shares/views/denominators remain blank.
4. Collect only permitted public fields or authorized exports. Check applicable platform terms and dataset license. Do not bypass logins, anti-bot controls, access restrictions, or prohibited scraping. Do not collect commenter identities.
5. Keep comparable date windows and metric observation ages where possible. Record paid/organic status, campaign context, follower observation time, and any unobserved confounders in manifest notes.
6. Audience claims require actual audience geography. Pakistani official accounts establish market relevance only. Ask for authorized aggregate audience evidence if segment analysis is required; caption language is not a demographic.
7. Import, review mapping, inspect invalid inputs and conflicting IDs, then export the evidence bundle. Report missingness and n before interpreting results.
